# Phase 7E College Connection Specification

Status: **CONNECTION ENGINE COMPLETE — CONNECTION REVIEW PENDING**

## Boundary

The Phase 7E connection engine is deterministic, offline, and review-only. It
consumes the exact approved Phase 7B college envelope and does not collect
network evidence, match identities by name, grant project-owner approval, or
load candidates into producer workflows.

COLLEGE-007 remains incomplete until the project owner reviews the candidate
wording and the approved candidates pass the later application-integration
acceptance checks.

## Identity and evidence versioning

### Stable connection identity

`connection_id` is derived from the relationship type, exact AUSL player IDs,
and canonical program IDs. It identifies the same conceptual relationship
across rebuilds and does not depend on the rendered sentence.

### Evidence version hash

`evidence_version_hash` separately binds the wording, season scope, source
IDs, source-record IDs, complete source-provenance fingerprints, completeness
states, review state, approval state, and uncertainty fields. A wording or
evidence change therefore cannot masquerade as the previously reviewed
version.

Producer eligibility is derived from review state, approval state, and the
absence of suppressions or uncertainty. It is not an independently editable
field. The candidate engine is prohibited from creating project-owner
approval.

## Supported evidence-backed relationships

- Transfer: requires at least two exact ordered school stints, exact seasons,
  verified identity, and verified field-level sources.
- Shared program at different times: requires the same canonical program and
  exact non-overlapping attendance ranges for both players.
- Championship teammates: requires exact same-program, same-year,
  team-scoped championship records for both players.
- Shared award: requires exact verified award labels after only a leading
  four-digit award year is normalized away.

The current checked-in evidence supports four transfers, two shared-program
connections, one championship-teammate connection, and one shared-award
connection.

## Fail-closed rules

- Overlapping attendance does not prove a teammate relationship. An explicit
  roster or teammate source is required.
- Missing attendance seasons suppress shared-program and transfer wording.
- Unverified source evidence, ambiguous identity, or a Needs Review résumé
  suppresses the relationship.
- Similar school names are not general name matches. Only exact program IDs or
  the explicitly defined `review-program-<display-name-slug>` Phase 7E alias
  may converge.
- College opponent, conference, coach, homecoming, WCWS, and college-to-AUSL
  role-change claims remain unavailable when the accepted envelope lacks the
  required typed evidence.
- Suppression never becomes approval and never produces producer copy.

## Determinism and audit trail

Candidate and suppression ordering is stable. JSON output is deterministic
UTF-8/LF and strict-load validation rejects unknown fields, duplicate IDs,
tampered stable IDs, tampered evidence hashes, unsupported schema versions,
and approval shortcuts. Every retained candidate and every suppression has a
machine-readable evidence or diagnostic record.

The complete review artifact is generated by
`tools/build_phase7e_connections.py` from explicit input and output paths.
Fact, UI, session, refresh, What Changed, and distribution integration remain
behind the current project-owner wording gate.
